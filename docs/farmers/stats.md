# Farming Stats

Villagers have Farming stats (range `-100` to `+100`) that influence manual farming behavior.

## Motivation

Adjusts the daily time window where manual farming is active.

## Efficiency

Chance to consume fewer or more seeds/bonemeal. Server config clamps this and includes a safety cap to avoid “infinite planting”.

## Plant Whisperer

Periodically bonemeals nearby crops during manual farming based on a configurable chance.

## Ranger

Modifies the villager’s maximum allowed farming range around its workstation.
