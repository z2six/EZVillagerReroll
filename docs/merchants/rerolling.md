# Rerolling & Locking

## Rerolling

Rerolling replaces one or more trade offers on a villager in exchange for emeralds.

## Locking offers

Locking an offer marks it as protected. Locked offers remain unchanged during rerolls and auto-search.

## Notes

- Costs and limits are server-configurable.
- Some flows may show “locked” outlines/markers in the UI to make it obvious what will be preserved.
